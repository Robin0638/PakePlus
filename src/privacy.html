Privacy Policy

Welcome to the "Treanly(Desktop Version)" application. We highly value your privacy protection and personal information security. This privacy policy aims to explain how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information.

1. Information Collection

We use local management and do not collect the following information on our servers:

  • Basic Information: The username you provide
  • Usage Data:
    • Focus time records
    • Task completion status
    • Points earning records
    • Badge unlock status

2. Information Usage

We hope you provide feedback to help us:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Personalize your user experience
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve our services
  • Provide user support and feedback
  • Send important notifications and updates

3. Information Storage

We store your data in the following ways:

  • Local Storage: Most data is stored locally on your device, ensuring quick access and offline use
  • Export Backup: All content can be exported or synchronized to the cloud by you to prevent data loss
  • Data Encryption: All transmitted and stored data is encrypted

4. Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your data security:

  • Using encryption technology to protect data transmission and storage
  • Conducting regular security assessments and updates
  • Limiting employee access to user data
  • Establishing data breach emergency response mechanisms

5. Your Rights

As a user, you have the following rights:

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account and data
  • Export your data
  • Choose whether to receive notifications

6. Minor Protection

We take the protection of minors' personal information very seriously. If you are under 18 years of age, please use our services under the guidance of a guardian.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated policy will be posted on this page, and you will be notified through in-app notifications for significant changes.
